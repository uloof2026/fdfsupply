Services

Toll Freeze-Drying

Food will degrade over time, and freeze-drying is a great preservation solution to create new market opportunities. We provides toll freeze-drying services with customers provided IQF materials.

Ingredients Supply

We produce and offer many kinds of size and specifications of freeze dried fruits, vegetables, as well as candies, smooties and etc in bulk as ingredients for industrial manufacturing.

New Formula Development

About Us

we are specialized in freeze-dried products.

Committed to premium, healthy, nutritious food by using innovative freeze-drying technology.

We specialize in freeze-dried food production. Our facility is located in Shandong - China's key agricultural province. Sited at the heart of raw material origins, we leverage our supply chain strengths to minimize raw material costs. Supported by abundant fruit and vegetable planting bases, we monitor every procedure from harvest, sorting and cleaning to quick-freezing and freeze-drying. With strict full-process quality control, we ensure our products are delivered to all corners of the world with reliable safety and consistent quality.
